Viybra – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Viybra ("we", "our", "us", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Viybra collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information when you use the Viybra mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Viybra, we may collect the following information:

  • Account and authentication information
  • Name and profile information
  • Age and gender information where provided
  • Profile photo or avatar information
  • Chat messages and communication information
  • Voice and video call related information
  • Coins, purchases and transaction information
  • Wallet and withdrawal information
  • UPI ID when required for withdrawals
  • Customer support communications
  • Reports, blocks and safety-related information
  • Device and technical information
  • Information necessary to maintain security and prevent fraud

2. Authentication

Viybra may use third-party authentication services, including Google Sign-In or other supported authentication providers, to allow users to create and access their accounts.

Authentication providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

3. Profile Information

When you create or update your Viybra profile, you may provide information such as your name, age, gender, profile image, avatar, interests, or other profile information.

Some profile information may be visible to other Viybra users as part of the application's social and communication features.

4. Chat, Voice and Video

Viybra provides communication features such as chat, voice calls, and video calls.

When you use these features, information required to establish and operate the communication may be processed by Viybra and relevant third-party communication service providers.

5. Microphone and Camera

Viybra may request access to your device microphone when you use voice communication features.

Viybra may request access to your device camera when you use video communication features.

These permissions are used for the communication features that require them.

6. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through Viybra to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide profile and user discovery features
  • Enable chat functionality
  • Enable voice and video communication
  • Manage coins and paid features
  • Process wallet and withdrawal requests
  • Provide customer support
  • Handle reports and blocking
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity
  • Improve application performance and reliability
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

7. Third-Party Services

Viybra may use third-party service providers to operate certain application features.

These services may include authentication providers, database and hosting services, realtime communication providers, payment providers, analytics or other infrastructure services.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

8. Voice and Video Communication Provider

Viybra may use a third-party real-time communication provider to provide voice and video calling functionality.

Information necessary to establish and operate calls may be processed by the communication provider.

9. Payments, Coins and Wallet

Viybra may provide coins, paid communication features, wallet functionality and withdrawal requests.

Payment-related information may be processed by applicable payment providers.

Where withdrawal functionality is available, users may be required to provide payment information such as a UPI ID.

10. Data Sharing

Viybra does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide application functionality, operate communication services, process payments, maintain security, prevent fraud, provide support, or comply with legal obligations.

11. User Safety

Viybra provides safety-related features such as reporting and blocking where available.

Information related to reports, blocks, abuse or safety incidents may be processed to investigate violations and protect users.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, enforce our policies, and comply with legal obligations.

14. Account Deletion

Viybra provides users with a method to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

When an account deletion request is completed, personal information associated with the account will be deleted or anonymized where applicable, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

15. Children's Privacy

Viybra is intended for adults and is not directed toward children. Children who do not meet the applicable minimum age requirements must not create or use a Viybra account.

If we become aware that information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable requirements, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation.

16. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the features available in Viybra, you may be able to update your profile information, manage permissions, block users, report users, or request account deletion.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
