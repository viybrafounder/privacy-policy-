BAE – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

BAE ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the BAE mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BAE, we may collect:

  • Account and authentication information
  • Profile information such as name, age, gender, avatar and profile details
  • Chat messages and communication information
  • Voice and video call related information
  • Coins, purchases, transactions and wallet information
  • UPI ID or payment-related information when you request withdrawals
  • Device, app and technical information
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support
  • Reports, blocks, feedback and safety-related information

2. Microphone and Camera

BAE may request access to your device microphone and camera when you use voice or video communication features. These permissions are used only for the communication features that require them.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide profiles and user discovery features
  • Enable chat, voice and video communication
  • Process coins, purchases and wallet transactions
  • Process withdrawal requests
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent abuse, fraud and misuse
  • Handle reports and blocking
  • Improve app security, reliability and performance
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

4. Third-Party Services

BAE may use third-party service providers to operate certain features of the application. These services may include authentication, database and realtime services, voice/video communication services, payment services and other infrastructure providers.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

5. Voice and Video Communication

BAE uses third-party real-time communication technology to provide voice and video calls. Information necessary to establish and operate a call may be processed by the relevant communication service provider.

6. Payments and Coins

BAE may provide paid features, coins and wallet-related functionality. Payment information may be processed by applicable payment providers. We do not intend to store complete payment card information on our own servers unless specifically stated otherwise.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide app functionality, process payments, operate communication services, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our policies.

10. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your BAE account and associated personal information through the account deletion option provided by BAE.

Some information may be retained where required by law, for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

BAE is intended for adults and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly allow children to create or use accounts. If we become aware that information has been collected from a child, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation.

12. User Safety

BAE provides safety features such as reporting and blocking. Users can report inappropriate behavior or content and block other users where these features are available.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
